## 2. The "What is it" block (≤120 words)

> Techsari is a scholarship matching platform built exclusively for African students. Instead of scrolling directories and guessing eligibility, you create a profile once — nationality, degree level, field of study, GPA, age, English status — and Techsari checks it against every listing's exact criteria. You get a ranked list of what you actually qualify for, a deadline tracker from Saved to Awarded, a document vault whose AI extraction reads your transcripts and CVs, and an essay studio that drafts, critiques and polishes your writing. A daily crawler surfaces new opportunities for human verification before they're published. Free to start.
