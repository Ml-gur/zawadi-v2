## Scholarship search built around you.

Most scholarship sites make you search through hundreds of listings and figure out the fine print yourself.

Zawadi does the matching for you.

Tell us who you are and what you want to study. We compare your profile with each scholarship's actual eligibility requirements and show you the opportunities that fit.

- **Your nationality** — Know exactly which country-specific and Africa-wide scholarships you can access.
- **Your academic profile** — Degree level, field, grades and other academic requirements are checked against each listing.
- **Your requirements** — English tests, work experience, age limits, publications, leadership and other conditions are captured instead of hidden in the fine print.

A scholarship does not disappear from Zawadi just because it requires IELTS, a high GPA, five years of experience or a research proposal.

Those requirements are data.

- **Clear eligibility** — See exactly why you match, what you are missing, and which requirements need attention.
- **Official sources** — Every scholarship is linked to the institution or organization that actually provides it.
- **Deadlines that matter** — Keep track of opening dates and application deadlines so an opportunity does not pass you by.

Zawadi shows you what each opportunity actually covers, so you can understand the real value before you apply.
